Call Centre or Contact Centre?

Which Do You Need?

Both run on the same KomTek platform — the difference is how many channels your team handles.

Call Centre

Designed around the phone. Ideal for sales and support teams handling large numbers of inbound and outbound calls who need fast routing, queues and dialling tools.

Contact Centre

Every channel in one place — calls, email, web chat, SMS, WhatsApp and social media — so customers get the same quality of service wherever they get in touch.

Inbound

Powerful Inbound Call Handling

Get callers to the right person quickly and keep them informed while they wait.

Automatic Call Distribution (ACD)

Spread incoming calls fairly across available agents using longest-idle, round-robin or ring-all strategies.

Skills-Based Routing

Match each caller with the agent best equipped to help — by language, product knowledge or seniority.

Interactive Voice Response (IVR)

Professional self-service menus that identify what the caller needs before they reach an agent.

Number-Based Routing

Route calls differently depending on which number or campaign the customer dialled, so you know where every lead came from.

Time Frames & Overflow

Separate rules for opening hours, lunch, evenings and bank holidays, with overflow to another team, site or AI agent when queues build.

Call Queues

Position announcements, on-hold music and callback options keep callers informed rather than frustrated.

Outbound

Dialling Tools for Sales & Collections Teams

Spend more time talking and less time dialling. Automated diallers are available on dedicated call-centre packages with our written approval.

Auto Dialler

Works through a contact list automatically, removing the need for agents to key in numbers by hand.

Predictive Dialler

Paces outbound calls based on expected agent availability to cut idle time between conversations.

Preview Dialler

Shows the agent the customer’s details first, so they can prepare before the call is placed.

Supervisors & Quality

Live Visibility and Quality Control

Real-Time Dashboards & Wallboards

Queue lengths, wait times, calls answered and agent status on screen as it happens.

Supervisor Panel

Call Recording

Record calls for training, quality assurance and dispute resolution, with secure storage.

Quality Assurance

Score recorded calls against your own standards and track improvement over time.

Agent Performance Management

Track handling time, outcomes and availability to recognise top performers and support others.

Call History & Reporting

Full records of answered, missed and outbound calls for smarter planning and decisions.

Unlimited Calling and Fair Usage for Call Centres

Unlimited-minute packages suit many outbound teams, but “unlimited” describes the pricing, not unrestricted use. Each user or agent seat includes up to 3,000 minutes of eligible UK landline and mobile calls per month. Agents on call-centre packages may dial manually; auto, predictive and bulk dialling require our written approval.
