Call Centre VoIP Solutions: A Complete Guide for UK Businesses

Whether you run a ten-seat sales team or a busy customer service operation, a cloud VoIP phone system gives your call centre the routing, flexibility and reporting it needs — without expensive on-site hardware.

Call centres live and die by their phone system. Calls need to reach the right agent quickly, nobody should wait longer than necessary, managers need visibility of what’s happening, and agents increasingly need to work from anywhere. Traditional on-premise PBX systems and ISDN lines struggle with all of that — and with the UK’s analogue and ISDN networks being switched off, many operations are moving to cloud-hosted VoIP.

What is a call centre VoIP solution?

A call centre VoIP solution is a cloud phone system designed for teams that handle high volumes of inbound or outbound calls. Calls travel over your internet connection rather than traditional phone lines, and all of the intelligence — queues, routing rules, users, numbers and reporting — lives in the cloud and is managed from a web portal. Agents can use desk phones, a softphone on their computer or a mobile app.

Key features every call centre needs

Call queues and intelligent routing

Call queues hold callers in order and distribute them to available agents, with announcements and hold music so callers know they haven’t been forgotten. Routing rules send calls to the right team based on the number dialled, the option chosen or the time of day.

Auto-attendant (virtual receptionist)

A professional menu — “press 1 for sales, 2 for support” — directs callers to the right department before they reach an agent, reducing transfers and handling time.

Time-frame rules and overflow

Different routing for opening hours, lunch, evenings and bank holidays, plus overflow to another team or site when queues get busy.

CRM integration

Caller preview, click-to-dial and automatic call activity logging mean agents see who’s calling before they answer and never have to type up a call manually. CRM & automation tools can then trigger follow-up emails and SMS.

Multi-user administration

Add and remove agents, change queue membership, record greetings and update routing yourself from an admin portal — essential when shifts and staffing change week to week.

Remote and hybrid agents

Because the system is in the cloud, an agent working from home has exactly the same capability as one in the office: the same queues, the same number presentation and the same CRM integration.

Local and international numbers

Present local numbers in the areas or countries you serve to increase answer rates and build trust. KomTek’s platform covers 78 countries and 3,723 area codes.

Benefits of VoIP for call centres

  • Lower costs — no on-site PBX hardware to buy or maintain, and predictable per-seat pricing
  • Scalability — add seats for a new campaign or seasonal peak in minutes, and remove them afterwards
  • Resilience — calls can be diverted to another site or mobiles if one location loses connectivity
  • Flexibility — support office, remote and hybrid agents on one system
  • Better customer experience — shorter waits, fewer transfers and agents who know who’s calling

Unlimited calling and fair usage: what call centres need to know

Unlimited-minute packages are attractive for outbound teams, but “unlimited” describes the pricing structure, not unrestricted usage. Every reputable provider has a fair usage policy, and it matters more for call centres than for any other type of business.

Under KomTek’s Fair Usage Policy, each user or agent seat includes up to 3,000 minutes of eligible UK landline and mobile calls per monthly billing period. Agents on call-centre or agent-seat packages may make outbound calls manually. Predictive or automated dialling, robocalling, bulk calling campaigns and call-through or resale are not permitted without our written approval — so if your operation uses a dialler or expects very high volumes, talk to us first and we’ll design the right package.

Planning a campaign? Tell us your expected agent count, call volumes and destinations up front. It ensures you’re on the right tariff and avoids any surprises.

Network and equipment requirements

  • Reliable business broadband or a leased line with enough upload capacity for your peak number of simultaneous calls
  • Quality of service (QoS) on your router to prioritise voice traffic over downloads and video
  • Wired connections for desk phones and agent PCs where possible
  • Good headsets — the single biggest factor in agent call quality and comfort
  • A backup plan such as a secondary connection or mobile failover for business continuity

Adding AI to your call centre

AI is now a practical addition to call centre operations. An AI receptionist can answer overflow and out-of-hours calls, handle routine questions, book appointments and qualify leads before they reach an agent — reducing queue times and freeing agents for complex conversations. A website AI chatbot can deflect simple enquiries before they become calls at all.

Checklist: choosing a call centre VoIP provider

  1. Does it support call queues, routing and time-frame rules?
  2. Can it integrate with your CRM?
  3. Can supervisors manage users and queues themselves?
  4. Does it support remote agents on softphones and mobile apps?
  5. Is the fair usage policy clear, and does it fit how your team actually dials?
  6. What uptime does the provider commit to, and where are its data centres?
  7. Can you keep your existing numbers?
  8. Is there UK-based support when you need it?

Frequently asked questions

Can call centre agents work from home on VoIP?

Yes. Cloud VoIP lets remote agents use a softphone, mobile app or desk phone with the same queues, numbers and CRM integration as office-based agents.

Is unlimited calling really unlimited for call centres?

No provider offers truly unrestricted use. KomTek unlimited packages include up to 3,000 minutes per user per month of eligible UK calls, subject to our Fair Usage Policy. Automated or predictive dialling requires written approval.

Can we keep our existing call centre numbers?

Yes. KomTek supports number porting, so you can transfer your existing geographic or 03 numbers at no extra charge.
