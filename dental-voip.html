Dental Practice Telephony

VoIP Phone Systems for Dentists & Dental Practices

Patients judge your practice by how easily they can reach you. KomTek’s cloud phone system makes sure every call is answered, routed and followed up — during surgery hours and after.

A Practice Phone System Built Around Patient Care

For most dental practices the telephone is still the front door. New patients call to register, existing patients call to book, reschedule or ask about treatment, and emergencies need a fast, calm response. When calls go unanswered or patients are left on hold, appointments are lost and trust suffers.

KomTek’s VoIP for dentists replaces traditional phone lines with a flexible cloud phone system that is affordable to run and easy to manage. Your reception desk, surgeries, back office and branches all share one system — with professional features that help your team handle busy periods, lunch breaks and out-of-hours calls without needing an outsourced call centre.

Useful Features

Dental VoIP Features That Make a Difference

Designed for the daily realities of a busy dental surgery.

Time-Based Greetings & Routing

Record different greetings for opening hours, lunch and evenings, and route calls automatically — to reception, another branch or an on-call mobile.

Automatic Call Diversion

If your practice internet goes down, calls are diverted to mobiles or another location so patients can still get through.

Practice Caller ID

Show your main practice number when staff call patients back — even from a mobile using the KomTek app.

Call Transfer

Pass a call from the front desk to a surgery, a private room for a sensitive conversation, or a colleague at another site.

Ring Groups

Ring the front desk and back office phones together so calls are picked up quickly during busy periods.

Voicemail-to-Email & Transcription

Voicemails are transcribed and sent to your inbox, giving you a written record of every message.

Manage From Anywhere

Change call settings, update greetings and manage users from any device through the online portal and app.

Auto-Attendant & Call Queues

A virtual receptionist menu (“press 1 for appointments”) and queues that keep patients informed rather than hearing an engaged tone.

99.999% Uptime

Hosted in UK data centres with multi-node redundancy for dependable patient communications.

Every Missed Call Is a Missed Patient

Your reception team is incredible — but they can’t be everywhere at once. When the phone rings during a busy morning, when a patient texts at 9pm, when someone messages on a Sunday — who’s answering?

Pair KomTek dental VoIP with an AI receptionist for dental practices and every call, text and chat gets an immediate, professional response — while your team focuses on in-clinic care.

Voice + Chat + SMS

One AI Receptionist. Every Channel Covered.

Whether patients call, text, chat on your website, or message on social media — your AI receptionist handles it all from a single, unified platform.

AI Voice Agent — Inbound Calls

Answers every inbound call with a natural, human-like voice. It understands the patient’s request, answers common questions about treatments, pricing, and availability — then books appointments or routes urgent calls to your team.

AI Voice Agent — Outbound Calls

Automate appointment confirmations, recall reminders, and follow-up calls — confirm upcoming visits, re-engage lapsed patients and run post-treatment satisfaction checks.

AI Chatbot — Website & Social

Engages website visitors 24/7, answers treatment questions, captures leads, triages emergencies, and submits appointment requests directly into your lead pipeline.

AI SMS & WhatsApp Agent

Patients can text your clinic number and receive instant replies — appointment requests, directions, FAQs and reminders via the channels patients already use.

Smart Appointment Booking

Checks real-time availability, suggests suitable slots, handles rescheduling and cancellations, and sends instant confirmations via SMS or email.

CRM & Lead Pipeline Integration

Every call, chat, and text conversation flows into your CRM pipeline automatically, tagged by treatment interest with follow-up tasks for your team.
