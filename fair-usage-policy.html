Fair Usage Policy

KomTek provides unlimited-minute packages subject to this Fair Usage Policy (FUP). The term “unlimited” describes the package’s pricing structure and does not mean that usage is unrestricted. The allowance and safeguards below are intended to support normal business communications and protect the quality, availability and fair use of the service.

1. Monthly minute allowance

Each individual user or agent seat includes up to 3,000 minutes of eligible calls during each monthly billing period. The allowance applies per user and is not pooled or transferable between users unless KomTek has expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

The allowance applies to eligible UK landline and mobile calls included in the customer’s chosen package. Any destinations, call types or services not expressly included in the order or service agreement are excluded and may be chargeable at the applicable rates.

2. Permitted business use

The service is intended for genuine, person-to-person business communications. Where a customer has purchased a call-centre or agent-seat package, agents may make outbound calls manually using the platform, subject to the agreed service specification and this policy.

3. Usage not permitted without written approval

Unless KomTek has expressly authorised the activity in writing, customers must not use the service for:

  • Predictive dialling, automated dialling, robocalling or systems that automatically initiate or generate calls.
  • Bulk calling, mass-calling campaigns or continuous high-volume calling that is inconsistent with the agreed service design.
  • Call-through, resale, onward supply, traffic aggregation or use as a carrier or wholesale termination service.
  • Call forwarding, bridging or trunking arrangements designed to allow multiple third parties or systems to use a single user allowance.
  • Artificial, fraudulent, abusive or otherwise unlawful traffic, or any activity that may compromise network integrity or service quality.
  • Calls to premium-rate, special-service, international or other excluded destinations where those destinations are not included in the customer’s package.

4. Monitoring and unusually high usage

KomTek may monitor call volumes, duration, destination patterns and other service data to check compliance with this policy, investigate suspected misuse and maintain service quality. Usage materially above the expected level for the customer’s stated business requirements, or a significant change in calling patterns, may trigger a review.

Where usage approaches or exceeds the 3,000-minute monthly allowance per user, KomTek may contact the customer to discuss the usage and the appropriate service arrangement. The customer should notify KomTek in advance if it expects a material increase in call volumes or a change in the nature of its operation.

5. Action where the policy is exceeded or breached

If KomTek reasonably considers that usage exceeds the allowance, is inconsistent with normal business use, creates a risk to the service or breaches this policy, KomTek may take proportionate steps, subject to the customer’s agreement and applicable law. These steps may include:

  • Reviewing the customer’s requirements and proposing a revised package, usage allowance or tariff.
  • Agreeing additional usage charges or a revised commercial arrangement in writing before applying them.
  • Restricting or suspending affected services where reasonably necessary to prevent misuse, protect the network or address a serious breach.
  • Terminating or declining to renew the affected service in accordance with the customer’s contract.

KomTek will, where reasonably practicable, discuss the issue with the customer before taking action. Nothing in this policy limits any rights or remedies expressly set out in the signed service agreement.

6. Customer responsibilities

  • Use the service only for lawful business purposes and in accordance with applicable telecommunications, privacy and direct-marketing laws.
  • Keep user credentials secure and ensure that each user or agent seat is used by the authorised individual.
  • Provide accurate information about the expected call volumes, destinations and intended use of the service.
  • Obtain any required consent and maintain appropriate records for outbound calling campaigns.
  • Tell KomTek promptly about suspected fraud, compromised credentials or unexpected call activity.

7. Changes to this policy

KomTek may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in its services, network requirements, legal obligations or reasonable usage standards. Where a change materially affects a customer’s service, KomTek will provide notice in accordance with the applicable contract and law.

8. Relationship with the service agreement

This policy should be read together with the customer’s order, service agreement and applicable tariff. If there is any inconsistency, the signed service agreement will take priority to the extent of that inconsistency, unless it expressly states otherwise.
