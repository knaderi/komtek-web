Get Found on Google Maps and in the Local Pack

When customers search for a service “near me”, Google shows the local pack — three businesses on a map above the organic results. A fully optimised Google Business Profile (previously Google My Business) is the single most important factor in appearing there.

KomTek’s local SEO team optimises every element of your profile: primary and secondary categories, services, products, business description, attributes, opening hours, photos and Q&A. We then keep it active with weekly Google posts and a steady flow of genuine customer reviews.

Reviews that build trust

Reviews influence both rankings and buying decisions. We help you request reviews from happy customers and respond to every review professionally.

Consistent citations (NAP)

Your business name, address and phone number must match across directories and your website. We audit and fix citations to strengthen your local search rankings.
