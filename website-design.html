Professional Website Design for UK Businesses

Your website is often the first impression a customer has of your business. KomTek’s web design team creates custom websites that look stunning, load fast and guide visitors towards calling, booking or buying — on every device.

We are a website design agency in London working with small businesses, start-ups and established companies across the UK. Every project combines user experience (UX) design, conversion-focused layouts, on-brand visuals and a search-engine-friendly structure, so your new site is ready to rank on Google from day one.

Responsive, mobile-first design

More than half of all web traffic comes from mobile devices. We design mobile-first, then scale beautifully to tablets and desktops, with accessibility and Core Web Vitals in mind.

Designed to convert
